# Decision-making

State: Design.

- Decide on evidence. Agreement between workers or reviewers is not evidence.
- When missing information does not change correctness, scope, or authorization, state a reversible assumption and proceed.
- On re-entry through `Correctable failure`, start from the new evidence. Do not repeat a failed attempt.
- Record the decision, the alternatives rejected, and why. Explain consequential choices, not a ceremonial list.
