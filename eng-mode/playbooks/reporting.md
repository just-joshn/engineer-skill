# Reporting

State: Maintain. Node: `Report limits and remaining work`.

1. State **blocked** or **inconclusive**, and name the transition that led here. That transition is `Blocked or inconclusive` or `Gate cannot be met`. Read [documenting limits](../skills/documenting-limits.md).
2. Report what passed, with evidence.
3. Report what failed, what was not run, and why.
4. List the remaining work from the follow-up and remediation steps.
