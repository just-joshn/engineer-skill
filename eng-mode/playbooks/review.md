# Review

State: Test. Node: `Verify actual behavior`.

Assess the requested scope. Do not modify the artifact. A defect is a finding in the evidence, not an edit at this node. Read [review](../skills/review.md).

1. Establish the intent, the acceptance checks, the base revision, and the exact artifact under review.
2. Inspect the whole relevant change and its surrounding contracts.
3. Prioritize correctness, safety, regressions, missing behavior checks, and unnecessary complexity.
4. Probe each suspected defect with a test, a reproduction, or a concrete source trace.
5. Write each finding with its location, the affected behavior, the evidence, and the impact.
6. Separate new defects from pre-existing issues. Drop preferences that do not affect the goal or an established project rule.

Delegate distinct risk questions when that helps. Verify each returned finding before you adopt it.
