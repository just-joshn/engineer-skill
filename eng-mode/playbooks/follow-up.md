# Follow-up

State: Maintain. Node: `Report limits and remaining work`.

1. Name what stopped the run. That is the failed gate, the blocked check, the inconclusive result, or the stop request. Read [issue triage](../skills/issue-triage.md).
2. Finish independent safe work that does not depend on the blocker. Leave the workspace in a known state.
3. Name the next concrete step, who can take it, and the evidence that would settle it.
