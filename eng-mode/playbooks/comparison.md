# Comparison

State: Design. Node: `Choose playbook; investigate and design`.

1. List the viable options or hypotheses and the criterion that decides between them. Read [option comparison](../skills/option-comparison.md).
2. Settle each difference by experiment or a focused prototype.
3. For a performance claim, record the workload, metric, environment, revision, and method. Capture a repeatable baseline before you change anything. Profile the limiting operation. Change one factor per treatment.
4. Compare under the same conditions. Repeat enough runs to separate an effect from noise.
5. Decide and record the choice, the rejected options, and the evidence. Read [decision-making](../skills/decision-making.md).
