# Comparison

State: Design. Node: `Choose playbook; investigate and design`.

1. List the viable options or hypotheses and the criterion that decides between them. Apply option comparison.
2. Settle each difference by experiment or a focused prototype where possible, not by preference.
3. For a performance claim, record the workload, metric, environment, revision, and method. Capture a repeatable baseline before changing anything. Profile the limiting operation. Change one factor per treatment.
4. Compare under the same conditions. Repeat enough to separate an effect from noise.
5. Decide and record the choice, the rejected options, and the evidence. Apply decision-making.
